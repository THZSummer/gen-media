## Two changes this round

1. **Restore `with crisp specular highlights where the light strikes`**.
   Comparing `BASE_H9_POSE` with phase-4's successful `BASE_H4_EN` sentence by sentence, R30 omitted this sentence —
   it is very likely the real switch for "the glaze being seen", not merely the defect list.
   Base prompt 1178 → 1234 characters (single variable, only this sentence added).
2. **Fix R30's framing mistake**: R30's square canvas is a **full body**, the face occupying only about 180 px, smoothed away by interpolation when magnified, so the glaze cannot be judged.
   Two of R31's three images are **tight portraits/three-quarter body** (R25 could reach conclusions back then precisely because it used a 2048² tight portrait).
   → For this the driver gains **per-image negative** capability: to force a full body, R28 put
   `portrait crop / close-up crop` into `NEG_H7`, which conflicts with a tight portrait; `NEG_H10_PORTRAIT` = `NEG_H6` + glow guard (removing the crop bans).

## Key conclusions

1. **Glaze sentence + defect list ≠ a fired glaze**: side by side at the same size as the R25 audit sheet (`out/macro-material/verdict-r25-vs-r31.png`):
   - R25: **hard glaze highlights** across the whole forehead/cheeks, a **glaze pull-back pinhole** on the upper forehead — an unmistakably ceramic surface
   - R31: real-human skin texture + satin sheen, **no hard glaze highlights, no pull-back pinholes**
2. **The material sentence really does change the skin** (proved by a strict same-seed ablation, not seed noise):
   R29 → R31 the skin goes from "warm-toned matte real-human skin" to "cool-toned, uniform, with glaze sheen".
   **The direction is right, but the strength is not enough.**
3. **The remaining difference is localized to "assertion strength"**: after a word-by-word comparison with `BASE_H4_EN`, the only untried key difference is
   - R25 writes `whose skin is **made of** bone china -- **the material itself, not a mask**`
   - R30/R31 write `Her skin is bone china -- ... **half real skin and half porcelain**`
   - R25 also has the closing sentence `Her face is human; her skin is porcelain.`; R30/R31 do not

   "half real skin and half porcelain" literally leaves her as **half a real person**, while the negative bans
   `human skin texture / pores / 真人皮肤 / 毛孔`, so the model resolves it as "glossy real-human skin".
   → Directly gives rise to [R32](r32.en.md) (change only the assertion, carrying over all of R31's seed/canvas/negative).
4. **Audit discipline is verified once more**: the reason R30 could not reach a conclusion was a **framing** problem of the audit sheet, not a canvas-size problem.
   For "microscopic glaze", a 1600² full body < a 2048² tight portrait.
