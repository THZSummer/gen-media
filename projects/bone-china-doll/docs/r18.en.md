## Key conclusions

1. **"A long clothing description pulls the camera in" is an inherent rule unrelated to the theme** — it reproduced three times across R10/R13 (European) and R17 (Eastern),
   and R18 obtained the full figure immediately after trimming the clothing list. **This can now be written into the methodology as settled**:
   > For a wide framing, **delete the clothing list**; for detail, **keep the material and craft nouns**. The two can coexist,
   > because the "clothing list" (crossed collar / wide sleeves / belt / cloud collar) and the "material craft" (gold weave / jade / gold filigree / pearls) are two different classes of text.
2. **Named jewellery is more reliable than "makeup"-type descriptions**: `璎珞`, `云肩`, `金镶玉带` and `步摇` landed first try;
   whereas `花钿` (a forehead ornament) was not drawn in either round — **concrete nouns with a definite object will be executed,
   while subtle facial makeup decorations are easily ignored**.
3. All three reached the R17 level or above; **quality did not fall back after the theme switch**.
