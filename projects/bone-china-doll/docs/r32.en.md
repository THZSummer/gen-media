## Changes this round (single variable, two subordinate clauses)

| | R25 (successful back then) | R30/R31 | **R32** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Material assertion | `whose skin is **made of** bone china -- **the material itself, not a mask**` | `Her skin is bone china -- ... **half real skin and half porcelain**` | **Switched to R25's sentence** |
| Closing sentence | `Her face is human; her skin is porcelain.` | ❌ None | **Added** |

**Why "half real skin and half porcelain" is a problem**: it literally leaves her as **half a real person**,
while the negative `NEG_H7` bans `human skin texture / realistic skin pores / 真人皮肤 / 毛孔 / matte skin`,
so the model settles on a compromise — "**glossy real-human skin**", exactly what R31 shows.

Base prompt 1234 → 1282 characters. **The Chinese layer is deliberately untouched** (it still says "skin half real, half bone china"):
that is the user brief's own wording, and changing it at the same time would be two variables; leaving it also makes it **a ready-made lever for the next round** (see conclusion 4).

## Key conclusions

1. **The material assertion is a dosage lever with adjustable levels** (same-seed three-step ladder, `out/macro-material/ladder-r29-r31-r32.png`):
   - **R29**: warm-toned matte **real-human skin**
   - **R31** (+ defect list + glaze sentence): cool-toned, **satin sheen**
   - **R32** (+ the assertion "the material is bone china"): cooler and more uniform, **clear glaze sheen**
   The three steps progress **monotonically** — proving this is not seed noise but the wording taking effect.
2. **R32 still differs from R25, but in a different direction** (`out/macro-material/verdict3-r25-r31-r32.png`):
   - R25: **hard glaze sheen** across the whole forehead + one **glaze pull-back pinhole** → close to **fully porcelain**
   - R32: **a soft overall glaze sheen**, no hard highlights, no pull-back pinholes → **half real, half bone china**
3. **This invites a question in return**: the user's brief is a "**half real, half bone china**" texture, and **R25 is actually more "porcelain" than the brief**.
   R29 leans to the real-human end, R25 to the fully-porcelain end, and **R32 lands in the middle leaning porcelain — quite possibly the best answer to this brief so far**.
   This needs the user to look at the images and decide; I cannot judge on their behalf.
4. **The remaining gap is localized and is a ready-made lever** (all untried):
   - **The Chinese layer still says "skin half real, half bone china"** — equivalent to saying "half a real person" again (deliberately left as the next variable this round)
   - `warm translucency glowing through the thin porcelain of the fingertips and the temples`
     (R25 has it; never restored because of the R24/R27 trap of "naming a part → that part becomes a light source")
   - `a soft blush of warm colour across the cheeks` (R25 has it; R27 localized it as the culprit of the "deliberate blush", so it was not restored.
     ⚠️ Note that R25 is a **porcelain context**, where the blush reads as "painting on porcelain", unlike R27's living-person context — the same sentence means opposite things in the two places)
   - `Her face is human; her skin is porcelain.` was added this round ✓
