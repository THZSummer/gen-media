> **Stage 3 begins**: the ethnicity and clothing culture change wholesale from European court to **Eastern classical**, while the material core is untouched.

## Change method: replace only the cultural elements, inherit the material core verbatim

R13 has proved that "the quality comes from the material vocabulary in `BASE_Q10`", so this round **does not invent a new quality baseline**;
instead it takes `BASE_Q10` apart sentence by sentence and **replaces only the culture-related sentences**:

| `BASE_Q10` sentence | Treatment | Result (`BASE_E`) |
|---|---|---|
| `Pristine flawless ivory porcelain ... dust-free` | **kept verbatim** | same (verified `in BASE_E == True`) |
| `Visible ball-joint seams at the neck and wrists` | **kept verbatim** | same |
| `An elegant princess face ...` | **replaced** | Eastern classical face: soft oval face, willow-leaf eyebrows, a fine straight nose bridge, cherry lips, slightly upturned almond eyes, dark brown irises |
| `A gown of real white lace ... pearl drop earrings` | **replaced** | ivory silk with a crossed collar and wide sleeves, gold-woven cloud-and-peony patterns, densely embroidered pearl edging, a **gold-set jade belt**, a **cloud collar**, gold-set jade earrings |
| `Fine art studio lighting ... neutral backdrop` | kept verbatim | same |
| `Extravagant costume craft: ... enamelled details` | **replace the material names** | gold-woven silk, densely embroidered silk grain, bead embroidery, **jade (soft waxen lustre + internal veining)**, gold filigree |
| `Sculptural chiaroscuro ... one strong key light ...` | **kept verbatim** | same (R13 proved this sentence safe) |
| `Extremely fine porcelain surface: ... sensor grain` | **kept verbatim** | same |
| `Jewellery realism: ...` / `Fabric realism: ...` | **replace the material names** | gold filigree / **jade internal veining** / pearl lustre; silk warp and weft / embroidered satin / linen lining |

> ✅ Residue check: `lace / tiara / sapphire / blue sash / european / western` are **all False** in `BASE_E`.

**Deliberately kept** (there is experimental evidence; no "incidental optimisation" allowed): perfect words such as `pristine / flawless / perfectly smooth`,
and `one strong key light`. **Deliberately avoided**: no standalone sharp lighting instruction, no standalone emphasis on eye size, no viewpoint/cropping wording.

## Key conclusions

1. **The theme switch landed in one round**: the approach of replacing only the cultural elements while keeping the material core **works** — the Eastern classical face, hair bun,
   crossed-collar gold weave, jade and gold filigree all landed as described, with **no loss** of the material realism that stage 2 worked so hard to establish.
2. **The old rule from R10/R13 holds under the Eastern theme too**: the `full-figure` prompt had a longer clothing description (crossed collar and wide sleeves + gold weave +
   cloud collar + jade belt), and the model again chose to "photograph the whole magnificent outfit", **refusing to pull back to a full figure**.
   → This shows the rule is unrelated to the theme and is Qwen's inherent reaction to a **long clothing description**.
3. **Props and scenes can be executed**: `round silk fan lying on the table` and `carved wooden folding screen`
   were both drawn accurately — unlike "cropping/viewpoint wording": **scene objects are "content that can be added" and will be executed;
   framing requirements are "constraints on the canvas" and will be ignored**. This is a new reading added this round.
