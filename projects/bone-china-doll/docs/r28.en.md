## Key conclusions

1. **"Position is weight" is the decisive lever for poses** (4th verification): the same pose sentence,
   placed after a long base prompt → completely ignored; placed at the very front + base prompt cut from 1802 to 635 characters → it immediately works.
   > **A pose is not obtained by adding words but by "reducing the descriptions competing with it".**
2. **The canvas lever is directional** (R27's qualification, further confirmed this round):
   an ultra-**portrait** canvas can force a taller subject (a standing full body); an ultra-**landscape** canvas **cannot** force a wider subject
   (the model fills both sides with background). A lying full body **can only come from wording**; the canvas is only responsible for not cropping it away (2.67:1 merely leaves room).
3. **Naming a part for translucency = that part becomes a light source** (2nd verification, after R24's elf ears):
   R24 `the ears` → elf pointed ears + orange translucency; R27 `the fingertips` → fingers and palms emitting orange light.
   > **Rule**: when writing "translucent", **do not specify which part it is**.
4. **A lying composition naturally reduces the "volume" of the clothing**: when lying, the gauze spreads into a flat surface, and the form of the crossed collar/yingluo/belt disappears
   (none of R28's three images shows the yingluo or crossed collar). → R29 restores them with a **lengthened minimal base prompt**.
