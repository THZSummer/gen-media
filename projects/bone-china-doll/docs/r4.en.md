## Three surgical changes to the prompt this round

| Change | Reason (from the R3 self-check) |
|------|---------------------|
| Delete all fixture nouns (formerly "large softbox key light") → change to `soft directional light from the upper left with a gentle rim light` + `backdrop free of any equipment` | R2/R3 **painted the softbox into the frame** |
| Add `elegant adult princess proportions, refined oval face, defined jawline, slender tapered neck, small head relative to the body, not a child` | The face shape kept skewing toddlerish |
| Pearls from multiple strands → `a single strand of pearls at the neckline`; also add `no floating objects, no extra limbs` | R3's pearl strand floated and broke, floating lace fragments |
| Translucency wording from `visible subsurface scattering` → `delicate warm translucency where the porcelain is thin at the rim of the ears and fingertips` | R3 image 2 grey-blue patch artifacts on the face |

**Remaining issues**:
1. **The face shape is still a "cute doll"** (big eyes, round face) — the word `doll` pulls the model toward a bébé doll prior
2. Image 2 pearls lean pink-orange; image 5 fingertips turned orange and the hands are rather slender and weak
3. Image 3 has small white spots on the chest and scattered white dots in the background
4. In the full-figure image (image 5) the skirt has a "moulded porcelain" texture and lacks lace fabric feel

## Changes for the next round (R5)

1. **Change the subject noun**: `doll` → `fine bone china figurine`, and explicitly write the adult features of `young woman in her early twenties` (almond eyes, eyelid crease, straight nose bridge, high cheekbones, closed lips)
2. Add a `proportionally sized` constraint to the eyes to suppress the "oversized eyes"
3. `steps 8 → 12`, verify the detail gain (cost is manageable at the same size)
4. Add `warm ivory pearls` to the pearls to fix the colour cast
5. For the full-figure image require `lace fabric with soft folds, not molded porcelain`
