## Project wrap-up conclusions

1. **The quality highland = R8–R10**, the final = R10 `window-light`; R11/R12 are degeneration rounds (causes located)
2. **The quality comes from the material/craft vocabulary in `BASE_Q10`** and reproduces stably across seeds; do not delete words for the sake of composition
3. **The lever for detail density is not resolution** (R14's negative result); resolution is only a composition knob
4. **Composition is controlled by the canvas aspect ratio** (R15); viewpoint/cropping wording is systematically ineffective
5. **When a composition cannot be obtained at the semantic layer, obtain it at the geometric layer** (true macro by cropping)
