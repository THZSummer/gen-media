> **Conclusion: R25 obtains both the "glaze feel" and the "micro traces"** — exactly what the user wants: "the skin should have a bone china texture, and it should be fine".
> The key move is **listing the porcelain-surface defects by name**, rather than writing a generic `microscopic texture`:
> `microscopic texture` gets read as "clean and smooth", whereas `pinholes / bubbles / drag lines / orange peel / uneven glaze`
> gets read as "**a surface with traces**".

## Key conclusions

1. **"Fine" ≠ "smooth"**. To get detail you must **give the model a defect checklist** (glaze pull-back spots, pinholes, glaze bubbles, polishing marks, orange-peel ripple,
   uneven glaze colour), and **explicitly forbid a uniform surface**. This is the most reusable item in this project regarding "detail".
2. **Do not name the ears for translucency**: `translucency of the ears` grows elf ears. Pointing at `temples / fingertips` instead is safe.
3. **A square canvas is the cheapest way to "get in close"**: 2048² doubles the pixels the face occupies, and only after cropping can the glaze pull-back spots be seen.
   (Compare R14's conclusion: raising resolution does not raise **whole-image** density, but it does raise the usable pixels of the **crop target** — the two are not contradictory.)
4. **The reproducible recipe for "half-real half-bone-china"** = glaze material block (up front) + real-human blood colour (`soft blush of warm colour`)
   + real-human hair (stray wisps) + defect checklist − uniform smoothness.

## Next round's changes (R26)

New user feedback: **the clothes must look light enough, with a "half-gauze half-bone-china" feel**.
R25's garment is a heavy **gold-woven round-medallion beizi** — the opposite of "light". R26 changes only the **garment layer**:
several cicada-wing layers of fine gauze over bone-china-white fine silk, thin enough to let the inner layer's light and shadow through, sleeves and hems floating as if about to lift;
the gauze's warp and weft fine, dense and crisp, the edges clean, **with the backbone of porcelain rather than limp thick cloth**;
remove the dense gold weaving, keeping only sparse silver-thread orchid sprays. The skin block and all sampling parameters are **frozen**.
