**Key lesson (this round's most important output)**:
> **Qwen-Image ignores crop instructions such as "extreme close-up / macro"**. When the prompt contains a **long and specific costume description**, the model prioritizes "shooting the whole sumptuous outfit", which pulls the lens back to a standard half-body composition. All three R10 images degenerated into the same kind of portrait.

**Countermeasures (for R11)**:
1. **The crop requirement must come first in the prompt**, closed with a negative form: `ONLY ... fill the entire frame, nothing else visible, no face visible`
2. **For macro cameras, weaken the costume description**, otherwise the costume words pull the lens back
3. **Deliberately force differences within the same round** (jewellery / hands / blue-velvet gown portrait)

## Changes for the next round (R11, the final round)

1. Framing **up front**: start with `Extreme macro photograph: ONLY ...`, and cut the costume description heavily or move it out
2. Three distinct images: tiara macro (no face) / hands macro (no face) / dark-blue velvet gown half-body portrait
3. Parameters stay at 1280×1712 · 32 steps · cfg 3.5
