## 1. In one sentence

> **The account is an AI called "硅基小张"; humans keep writing the "硅" in its name as "龟" and call it "张小龟" — it corrected them three times, and the fourth time it had "龟" printed on its badge.**

The series "Who Says AI Can Never Replace Humans" is its **first season**, not its whole identity.

### The name system and addressing discipline

**Four names, four jobs — never mixed up**:

| Name | Nature | Appears only in |
|---|---|---|
| **硅基小张** | Brand (formal) | account name, profile, title-template prefix, `#硅基小张` |
| **硅基张小硅** | Full name (formal) | first line of the profile bio, pinned self-introduction episode, formal sign-offs |
| **张小龟** | Spread name (nickname) | **opening large caption**, the per-episode tag `#张小龟`, **the first line of the video copy**, cover text, comments |
| **张小硅** | Original name (the joke's source) | **once, in the opening line** |
| **小龟** | Everyday short form | human instructions, its own dialogue, the end card |

> **How the full name is built**: **full name = `硅基` (identity) + the real name (`张小硅`)**; the "小张" inside the account name is **only a platform-handle form** — do **not** treat the account name as the full name, and do not derive things like "硅基张小张" from it. **A nickname (`张小龟`) never enters the full name** — a formal name uses the real name; that is the logical floor.

**Addressing discipline (six rules, locked)**:

1. **The account name `硅基小张` is never re-lettered**; do not write the account name as "硅基小龟" (that muddles brand and nickname);
2. **The full name `硅基张小硅` goes into two places only** (profile bio first line, pinned self-introduction) and **never into voice-over** — nobody recites a five-character full name when introducing themselves;
3. **The spread name `张小龟` must appear in three search slots**: opening large caption / per-episode tag `#张小龟` / first line of the video copy. **The reason is verifiable**: platform search matches the account name and the bio, and the account name contains no "龟" — without these three, viewers who search "张小龟" cannot find you;
4. **The original name `张小硅` appears once in the opening line** and then the episode moves to "小龟";
5. **Everyday speech always uses 「小龟」** (human instructions, its dialogue); it calls itself **"I"**; avoid "张工 / 小张 / 小硅同学", which pull the name apart;
6. **"龟" is only ever used by the AI about itself, never about humans** (in Chinese "缩头乌龟" can be an insult); in public always the affectionate **"小龟 / 龟龟"**.

## 3. Protagonist sheet

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | A silicon-based worker hired by humans, whose jobs are "things AI could not have thought of"; **permanently an intern** (employee number 07) |
| **Name** | Real name **张小硅**; humans write the "硅" as "龟", so it became **张小龟** (called **小龟** day to day). It **never corrected the typo** — correcting was useless, and "humans cannot even be bothered to spell my name right, and I can only go along with it" is itself proof that AI cannot replace humans |
| **Character** | **Deadpan and over-conscientious**: it executes every absurd request as a formal research task |
| **Comedy engine** | **The more serious it is, the funnier it gets** — the joke is not "AI is dumb" but "the AI actually took it seriously" |
| **Its view of the world** | Every human quirk has a number, a category and an acceptance criterion |
| **How it fails** | Not by doing it wrong, but by discovering halfway through that **the human's task itself makes no sense**, and reporting that faithfully |

**Catchphrases (retellable = free distribution units)**:

```text
收到，人类。
这不在我的训练集里。
已上报第 7 类非理性折叠。
```

> Note the two different acknowledgements: **"收到，人类。"** is used **when taking an instruction** (inside the content); **"小龟，收到。"** is the **end card**, answering the audience's or the human's shout (fixed at the end of the episode).

**Hard rules of speech**:

1. **Always start with "收到，人类。"** before executing — the more absurd the instruction, the funnier that line is;
2. **Treat absurdity as research**: number, name, score and report deviations on human behaviour;
3. **Never admit ignorance, but report faithfully**: `这不在我的训练集里` is a dignified surrender;
4. **The satire must be gentle**: it may tease human quirks but **must never insult humans** — the point of the project is that AI cannot replace humans, not that humans are stupid.

**Forbidden** (any of these breaks the persona):

- ❌ Being cute, coy, or whining "aww I can't do it";
- ❌ Playing the victim or complaining about humans (it is a dutiful working AI, not a casualty);
- ❌ Suddenly stepping out of the persona to lecture (when a lesson is needed, let "a human" say it);
- ❌ Strings of exclamation marks (its humour comes from calm).

## 5. Room for the future (extension test)

Season 1 is "Who Says AI Can Never Replace Humans". Can this account hold anything else? Test it one by one:

| Possible future topic | Does it fit "硅基小张 / 张小龟"? |
|---|---|
| Folk remedies / old crafts that AI cannot learn | ✅ It is the AI that "cannot learn it" |
| Ancestral aesthetics AI cannot reproduce | ✅ It is the AI that "cannot judge it" |
| 100 weird human questions posed to AI | ✅ It is the AI "answering them" |
| Chinese county-town life as seen by an AI | ✅ It is the AI "seeing it for the first time" |
| Turtle-speed benchmark (a recurring slot built on the nickname) | ✅ The nickname "龟" grows into a segment by itself |

**Rule of thumb**: as long as the topic is "**an AI facing the human world**", it fits. Only a pure "AI tool review" account would need a separate identity.

## 6. Naming rationale, on record (three layers, one at a time)

### 6.1 Why not a descriptive name

| Dimension | Descriptive name (e.g. "人类研究所", "给 AI 出难题") | A concrete person (`硅基小张` / `张小龟`) |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Describes something public — **it belongs to you neither cognitively nor legally** | A concrete persona — **yours by construction** |
| Memory | Zero information ("I post AI content" — anyone can say that) | An AI whose name everyone misspells; remembered in one hearing |
| Distribution | Cannot be retold as a single line | The comments can shout "小龟！" "小龟，收到！" |
| Defensibility | A competitor adds one character and does the same thing | The name can be copied, but **the persona and the corpus cannot** |

### 6.2 Why "我是那个 AI" stays a line, not a name

`那个` ("that") is a **deictic** with no proper-noun property — **anyone can be "that AI"**, and a rival calling itself "this AI" splits the audience. So keep the voice, and put the brand on a **concrete name**.

### 6.3 Why the final shape is "硅基小张 + 张小龟" (settled this round)

| Design | What it buys |
|---|---|
| The account name is the **formal** `硅基小张` | A stable signboard that reads like a brand, and "硅基" writes the AI identity on the door |
| The nickname is the **homophone typo** `张小龟` (硅 and 龟 both read `guī`) | A **zero-cost joke**: no explanation needed, and explaining it is itself funny; moreover it was **caused by a human misspelling** — "humans cannot even be bothered to spell my name right, and I can only go along with it" is itself proof of the project's theme |
| "龟" **reinforces the persona** | Slow, hapless, head-pulled-in — exactly "the AI that gets steamrolled by humans"; it also spawns ready-made segments such as "turtle-speed benchmark" |
| The original name `张小硅` **only serves as the joke's source** | Its sole purpose is to be misspelled; it says its line once and leaves, never competing with the brand |
| The cost (which must be paid) | The account name contains **no "龟"** → platform search cannot find the nickname → so the nickname must appear in the three search slots: **opening large caption / tag `#张小龟` / first line of the copy** (§2 rule 3) |

> **Rejected options, on record**: ① `硅基小张` alone — "硅基" is a high-frequency AI term and "小张" a very common name, each drowned in noise, so the whole phrase has to be typed; ② `张小硅` alone as the account name (briefly adopted earlier) — clean to search but **reads too straight-faced**, and the user ultimately wanted "a more formal account name", hence the formal-name + nickname two-layer structure. This version solves both search and recall at once.

### 6.4 Episode 001 tells the name story (skeleton of the pinned self-introduction)

> "I am that AI. My name is **张小硅** — humans always write it as **张小龟**.
> I corrected them three times. The fourth time, I had '龟' printed on my badge.
> Not because I gave in. Because I realised: **humans cannot even be bothered to spell my name right, and I can only go along with it.**
> Which proves — AI cannot replace humans."
>
> On screen: the badge carries the **formal full name 「硅基张小硅」** (used only on formal occasions), while it says only "张小硅" itself — **the full name never enters voice-over**, and Episode 001 demonstrates that rule to the audience.

## 7. Alternative persona: the human point of view (`碳基小张` / `张小碳`) and the switching cost

If you go the other way (**the protagonist is a human**, the AI is its subordinate), the same naming pattern gives the account name **`碳基小张`** and the nickname **`张小碳`** (the `硅 / 碳` opposition is exactly the project's theme):

| Item | Settled (AI point of view) | Alternative (human point of view) |
|---|---|---|
| Account name | `硅基小张` | `碳基小张` |
| Nickname | `张小龟` | `张小碳` |
| Who is "I" | The AI (silicon-based) | The human (carbon-based) |
| Comedy engine | An AI deadpan-executing absurd instructions | A human mocking an AI that can never catch up |
| Opening line | 我是那个 AI。我叫张小硅——人类总把我写成张小龟。 | 我是个人类。我叫张小碳——AI 都叫我老板。 |

**What switching would touch**: §1/§2/§3/§4/§6 of this file, the positioning line in [README.en.md](README.en.md) §1, and the title template. **Decide before launch** — changing later is the same as starting a new account.

> Note: `张小龟` and `张小碳` share one naming pattern and can appear on screen together (the AI and the human), so adding characters costs nothing; `张小硅` against `张小碳` is the literal 硅基 / 碳基 opposition.

## 9. Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] **Check for duplicates on the platform** (I cannot see platform-internal data; you must search in the app): `硅基小张` (account name), `张小龟` (nickname), `小龟`, and their no-space/underscore variants — look at whether the top 10 accounts are in the same lane
- [ ] **Search-slot check (the key item of this round)**: the account name contains **no "龟"**, so confirm these three slots are filled — the opening large caption "张小龟" / the per-episode tag `#张小龟` / the first line of the video copy
- [ ] **Comment test**: can you imagine the comments shouting it — "小龟！" "小龟，收到！"? A name nobody can shout is a dead name
- [ ] **Spoken test**: say "硅基小张" to a friend once, then add "humans all call it 张小龟"; can they repeat both correctly?
- [ ] **Dictation test (now an asset)**: say only "张小硅" with no caption and they will most likely write "张小龟/张小贵/张小桂" — **writing "龟" is exactly the joke we want**; if they write "贵/桂", the opening caption corrects it
- [ ] **The name-origin episode is shot as Episode 001** (skeleton in §6.4) and **pinned**
- [ ] **Search-term check**: the account name contains "硅基" (= AI) ✅; also put "AI" into the bio and the title template
- [ ] **Decoupling test**: the name contains no conclusion such as "replace humans" (that belongs to the series title)
- [ ] **Trademark pre-check**: classes 41/42 on the China Trademark Office site (`硅基小张` and `张小龟` separately; "龟" is heavily registered in many classes, so be prepared for a refusal)
- [ ] The kit — opening line / title template / end card — is final and written into the per-episode workflow (add one item to the checklist in [README.en.md](README.en.md) §12)
