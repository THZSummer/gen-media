## 1. In one sentence

> **The account is an AI called "张小硅" (a human-style name on a silicon-based identity). It is hired by humans to do work, speaks in the first person throughout, and reports the absurd requests it receives with complete seriousness.**

The series "Who Says AI Can Never Replace Humans" is its **first season**, not its whole identity.

### Addressing system and addressing discipline

| Who | How they address it | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Audience / platform search | **张小硅** (full name) | account name, title template, the **large on-screen caption** in the opening, hashtags |
| Humans (employer / task setter) | **小硅** | in every human instruction |
| Itself | **"I"**; the full name only when introducing itself | dialogue; **never** product-style forms like "小硅同学" or "AI 小硅" |
| End card | **"小硅，收到。"** | a fixed end card that catches the audience's shout |

**Addressing discipline (five rules, locked)**:

1. **The full name appears in only 4 places**: account name, title template, opening caption, profile/hashtags; in dialogue always use the **short form**;
2. Humans always address it as **「小硅」** (not "张工" or "小张" — those pull the name apart);
3. It refers to itself as **"I"** and **never uses its own full name** (nobody talks like that);
4. The opening **must render "张小硅" as a large on-screen caption**: people who only hear the name tend to write "张小贵/张小桂", and the caption is the only defence against mishearing;
5. **No** forms such as `小硅助手` / `AI 小硅` / `小硅同学`, which would echo real AI products (小爱/小度/豆包/Kimi…).

## 3. Protagonist sheet

| Item | Setting |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | A silicon-based worker hired by humans, whose jobs are "things AI could not have thought of"; **permanently an intern** (employee number 07) |
| **Name** | Surname 张, given name 小硅 — **a human-style name on a silicon-based identity**; that mismatch is the joke and needs no explanation |
| **Character** | **Deadpan and over-conscientious**: it executes every absurd request as a formal research task |
| **Comedy engine** | **The more serious it is, the funnier it gets** — the joke is not "AI is dumb" but "the AI actually took it seriously" |
| **Its view of the world** | Every human quirk has a number, a category and an acceptance criterion |
| **How it fails** | Not by doing it wrong, but by discovering halfway through that **the human's task itself makes no sense**, and reporting that faithfully |

**Catchphrases (retellable = free distribution units)**:

```text
收到，人类。
这不在我的训练集里。
已上报第 7 类非理性折叠。
```

> Note the two different acknowledgements: **"收到，人类。"** is used **when taking an instruction** (inside the content); **"小硅，收到。"** is the **end card**, answering the audience's or the human's shout (fixed at the end of the episode).

**Hard rules of speech**:

1. **Always start with "收到，人类。"** before executing — the more absurd the instruction, the funnier that line is;
2. **Treat absurdity as research**: number, name, score and report deviations on human behaviour;
3. **Never admit ignorance, but report faithfully**: `这不在我的训练集里` is a dignified surrender;
4. **The satire must be gentle**: it may tease human quirks but **must never insult humans** — the point of the project is that AI cannot replace humans, not that humans are stupid.

**Forbidden** (any of these breaks the persona):

- ❌ Being cute, coy, or whining "aww I can't do it";
- ❌ Playing the victim or complaining about humans (it is a dutiful working AI, not a casualty);
- ❌ Suddenly stepping out of the persona to lecture (when a lesson is needed, let "a human" say it);
- ❌ Strings of exclamation marks (its humour comes from calm).

## 5. Room for the future (extension test)

Season 1 is "Who Says AI Can Never Replace Humans". Can this account name hold anything else? Test it one by one:

| Possible future topic | Does it fit "张小硅"? |
|---|---|
| Folk remedies / old crafts that AI cannot learn | ✅ It is the AI that "cannot learn it" |
| Ancestral aesthetics AI cannot reproduce | ✅ It is the AI that "cannot judge it" |
| 100 weird human questions posed to AI | ✅ It is the AI "answering them" |
| Chinese county-town life as seen by an AI | ✅ It is the AI "seeing it for the first time" |

**Rule of thumb**: as long as the topic is "**an AI facing the human world**", it fits. Only a pure "AI tool review" account would need a separate identity.

## 6. Why not a descriptive name (rationale, on record)

| Dimension | Descriptive name (e.g. "人类研究所", "给 AI 出难题") | Proper noun (`张小硅`) |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Describes something public — **it belongs to you neither cognitively nor legally** | A coined proper noun — **yours by construction** |
| Memory | Zero information ("I post AI content" — anyone can say that) | A human-style name on a silicon-based identity; remembered in one hearing |
| Distribution | Cannot be retold as a single line | The comments can shout "小硅！" "小硅，收到！" |
| Defensibility | A competitor adds one character and does the same thing | The name can be copied, but **the persona and the corpus cannot** |

**Why "我是那个 AI" stays a line, not a name**: `那个` ("that") is a **deictic** with no proper-noun property — **anyone can be "that AI"**, and a rival calling itself "this AI" splits the audience. So keep the voice, and put the brand on a **proper noun**.

**Why `硅基小张` was replaced by `张小硅`** (on record so the discussion is not repeated):

| Problem | `硅基小张` | `张小硅` |
|---|---|---|
| Search | "硅基" is a **high-frequency term** in AI discourse and "小张" is a **very common name** — each is drowned in noise, so the whole phrase must be typed | Unique as a three-character string; viewers type the full name naturally, no ambiguity |
| Shape | A descriptive compound (silicon-based + Xiao Zhang), reads like a **title** | `张小X` is a Chinese personal-name pattern, reads like **a person's name** |
| Short form | "小张" is too generic and "硅基小张" too long (unusable in dialogue) | **「小硅」** — short, warm and one of a kind |

## 7. Alternative persona: 张小碳 (human point of view) and the switching cost

If you go the other way (**the protagonist is a human**, the AI is its subordinate), the name becomes **`张小碳`** — the same naming pattern as `张小硅`, and the `硅 / 碳` opposition is exactly the project's theme:

| Item | 张小硅 (**settled**) | 张小碳 (alternative) |
|---|---|---|
| Who is "I" | The AI (silicon-based) | The human (carbon-based) |
| Comedy engine | An AI deadpan-executing absurd instructions | A human mocking an AI that can never catch up |
| Opening line | 我是那个 AI。我叫张小硅，人类都叫我小硅。 | 我是个人类。我叫张小碳，AI 都叫我老板。 |

**What switching would touch**: §2/§3/§4/§6 of this file, the positioning line in [README.en.md](README.en.md) §1, and the title template. **Decide before launch** — changing later is the same as starting a new account.

> Note: the 硅/碳 opposition is deliberate — `张小硅` and `张小碳` can appear on screen together (the AI and the human), and they share one naming pattern, so adding characters costs nothing.

## 9. Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] **Check for duplicates on the platform** (I cannot see platform-internal data; you must search in the app): `张小硅`, `小硅`, and their no-space/underscore variants — look at whether the top 10 accounts are in the same lane
- [ ] **Comment test**: can you imagine the comments shouting it — "小硅！" "小硅，收到！"? A name nobody can shout is a dead name
- [ ] **Spoken test**: say it to a friend once; can they repeat it correctly the second time?
- [ ] **Dictation test**: say the name with no caption and see what they write down (most likely "张小贵/张小桂") → hence **the opening of every episode must render "张小硅" as a large caption**
- [ ] **Search-term check**: does the name keep a high-frequency search term? (`张小硅` contains no "AI" → **put "AI" into the bio and the title template** to compensate)
- [ ] **Decoupling test**: the name contains no conclusion such as "replace humans" (that belongs to the series title)
- [ ] **Trademark pre-check**: classes 41/42 on the China Trademark Office site
- [ ] The kit — opening line / title template / end card — is final and written into the per-episode workflow (add one item to the checklist in [README.en.md](README.en.md) §12)
