## 5. Distillation

107. **The donor's semantic category must match the function of the landing site**:
     at the landing site "add a pair of wings to the back", the donor must **itself be a wing, or carry flying semantics**;
     a "wing-like shape" alone is not enough (fish fins and samaras are also wing-shaped, but have no "can fly" semantics).
108. **Atlas-type sub-themes need a "baseline section"**: section 1 has no transplant piece;
     it is both the first page and the frame of reference for the following four sections (this atlas is isomorphic to horn-atlas).
