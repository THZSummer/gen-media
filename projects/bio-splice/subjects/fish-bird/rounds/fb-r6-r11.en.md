## 1. Why we switched to an "emergence stage" sequence

In practice only **wings** could land (tail feathers and feathers covering the back failed in both scenes),
and whether wings land **is determined by the waterline position**. So we replaced "swapping parts" with "the timeline of leaving the water":

| Round | Period | Emergence stage | Presentation | Finals |
|----|----|----------|------|------|
| R6 | 01 | **leaping out of the water** (just left the water, splash not yet fallen) | backlit splash · eye-level 100mm · landscape | 2 |
| R11 | 02 | **half out of water** (waterline crossing the body) | side light · eye-level 135mm · square | 3 |
| R8 | 03 | **fully out of water · both wings fully spread** | upper-side view · backlit rimmed feathers · square | 2 |
| R9 | 04 | **skimming low over water** | telephoto compression · flat light on grey water · landscape | 2 |
| R10 | 05 | **Kunpeng** (dusk sea, silhouette) | wide angle · backlit silhouette · landscape | 2 |

**Constant within a period**: one emergence moment + one fixed presentation per period;
**Different between periods**: waterline position, camera, light, and canvas all differ — the five periods together form one timeline.

**The finale "Kunpeng" holds up**: on a dusk sea a fish spreads a fully spread pair of bird wings,
almost a silhouette in the backlight — exactly the "transformed into a bird" line from *Xiaoyaoyou*.

## 3. Period 02's supplementary round (R11)

In R7's "half out of water" only one of the two landed (`fish-wings` was eaten by the waterline, `fish-wings-half` landed).
So R11 used the **already-validated phrasing** (half-opened, positioned higher) across three seeds, **all three landed**.
→ This also shows: **once the phrasing is settled, producing extra takes is cheap and stable** (Rule 84).

## 4. Takeaways

(Rules 85–87 are in [fb-r1-r5.md](fb-r1-r5.en.md) and [parts.md](../parts.en.md).)

88. **A "single usable part" can still carry a sub-theme**: as long as you treat the **independent variables of the presentation layer** (here the emergence stage)
    as the difference between periods, the five periods still each have an identity — there is no need to force a different part for every period.
