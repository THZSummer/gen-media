## 1. Concept: what is crossed is not the species but the **life form**

**Bark ↔ hide, root system ↔ feet, growth rings ↔ bone** ——
the **largest in scale** group within "biological splicing": plant ↔ animal, a splice of **two life forms**.

- **Base ＝ old tree** (when changing the base, use a **standing dead tree**)
- Donor ＝ beast parts: hide pattern K1 / feet K2 / horns K4 (~~eye K5~~ removed)

## 3. Negative conclusion: **beast feet land 0%**

After **zooming into the root-system region ×1.6 and reading them one by one**, confirmed: only the shape drifts, **the roots are still roots**. Two reasons:

1. **The landing site is a canonical structure** (the root system is a tree organ, Rule 99)
2. **A "foot" also needs a joint/limb as a bearing surface** —— a tree has no legs (Rules 74/97)

→ **Exactly the same shape** as dragon-nines' "eagle claws require a four-limbed base first".

## 4. Hide pattern: another case of "a property can be vacated"

R1/R2 same seed, same presentation, only the base wording changed:

| Base wording | Effect on K1 |
|----------|-----------|
| Occupied version: `rough fissured bark` | the bark "turns soft and yellow" (A=3) |
| **Vacated version**: do not describe the bark | an **obvious hide / fur band** appears on the trunk (A=4) |

→ Same shape as the insect body surface in cordyceps and the marginal teeth in flytrap (Rules 96/100).

## 5. The first time a base change "did not drop but held" (a supplement to Rule 102)

Period 04 changed the base from an **old tree** to a **standing dead tree**, and K1 + K4 **still work**;
whereas flower-bird's magnolia → lily dropped the hit rate from 5/5 to 1/2.

> **Conjecture**: **property replacement (hide pattern) and empty-face addition (beast horns) depend little on the base's form**;
> landing sites that need geometric fit (the flower centre) are more sensitive (Rule 106).
