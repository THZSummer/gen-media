# Tree-Beast Part Table (tree + beast)

**Design source**: **bark ↔ hide, root system ↔ feet, growth rings ↔ bone** ——
the **largest in scale** group within "biological splicing": what is crossed is not the species but the **life form** (plant ↔ animal).

**Base ＝ old tree** (many knots, fissured bark, thick roots, a huge trunk); when changing the base, use a **standing dead tree**.
Donor ＝ beast parts: hide pattern K1 / feet K2 / horns K4. ~~Eye K5~~ removed (a small part with no carrier).

---

## 1. Part table and measured results (**two parts work, one is rejected**)

| No. | Part | Landing site | Nature of landing site | Result |
|------|------|------|----------|------|
| K1 | Hide pattern | bark | **property** (grain) | ✅ works (vacated version 4.55 ×3, occupied version only 4.25) |
| K2 | Beast feet | root system | **structure** (and needs a joint/limb) | ❌ **land 0%** (after zooming into the root-system region the roots are still roots) |
| K4 | Beast horns | trunk | **empty face** | ✅ **fully works** (5.00 ×6, still works after the base change) |

## 2. Why K2 "beast feet" cannot land (negative conclusion)

After **zooming into the root-system region ×1.6 and reading them one by one**, confirmed: only the shape drifts, the roots are still roots. There are two reasons:

1. **The landing site is a canonical structure**: the root system is a tree organ (Rule 99);
2. **A "foot" also needs a joint/limb as a bearing surface** —— a tree has no legs (Rules 74/97),
   exactly the same shape as dragon-nines' "eagle claws require a four-limbed base first".

## 3. K1 hide pattern: another case of "a property can be vacated"

R1/R2 same seed, same presentation, only the base wording changed:

| Base wording | Effect on K1 |
|----------|-----------|
| Occupied version: `rough fissured bark` | the bark "turns soft and yellow" (A=3) |
| **Vacated version**: do not describe the bark | an **obvious hide / fur band** appears on the trunk (A=4) |

→ Same shape as the insect body surface in cordyceps and the marginal teeth in flytrap: **grain is a property and can be vacated** (Rules 96/100).

## 4. K4 beast horns: empty face + different form = the most stable part

The trunk is an **empty face**, and the horns grow out of the surface (Rule 97), and they are **different in form** from the tree shape (Rule 91);
they still work after the base change (old tree → standing dead tree) (two rounds ×2 takes all worked).
→ **The mainstay part of this sub-theme**.

## 5. Verifying the base change (Rule 102)

Period 04 changed the base from "old tree" to "standing dead tree": **K1 + K4, both parts still work on the new base**.
This is the first case where "the hit rate did not drop after a base change" ——
compare flower-bird (magnolia → lily, hit rate 5/5 → 1/2).
→ Conjectured to be related to **the nature of the landing site**: property replacement and empty-face addition depend less on the base's form.
