## 1. This round's methodological change: adding a **same-round base control**

R6/R7's objective metrics were flawed: the base control used an image from a **different round** (R1's pure eagle / pure cat), and a different camera masks the differences
— which is exactly why [r02](r02.en.md) said "global SSIM cannot separate success from failure".

So from R8 on, every round includes a control shot that **writes only the base with no part added** (`base-eagle` / `base-cat`):
same seed, same sentence skeleton, its only difference from the round's other shots being "part added or not".

## 3. Two new rules

### 1. Thumbnails lie (another confirmation of lesson 51)

`e-cat-ears-whiskers` looks in the 512 overview image as if "the whole head turned into a cat head";
only at **×2 magnification** is it clear that the eagle beak is still there, with cat ears, cat whiskers and a ring of cat cheek fur added.
I once misjudged it as "beyond expectations", and the magnified audit corrected that.

### 2. The constraint on stacking parts is not the count but **whether the anatomical regions conflict**

| Combination | Three landing sites | Result |
|------|----------|------|
| eagle base + C1 ears + C5 tail | head, **tail** | ✅ holds (period 02) |
| eagle base + C1 ears + C6 whiskers | head, head (two in the same region) | ✅ holds (period 04) |
| eagle base + C1 ears + C5 tail + C6 whiskers | head, **head, tail** | ❌ the model **draws a separate cat** to make up the difference (excluded from period 04) |
| cat base + E6 neck ruff + E5 tail feathers + E2 wings | neck, tail, back (the three do not contend) | ✅ holds (period 05) |

> **Rule correction** (a narrowing of [r03](r03.en.md) Rule 58):
> the earlier statement that "stacking multiple parts does not interfere" was **too broad**. In practice the constraint is the **region**:
> - at most **two** stacked in the same region;
> - once a region has to carry three, the model **draws a separate individual** to satisfy the extra one.
>
> **Practical advice**: when adding parts, place them in different anatomical regions where possible; never more than two in the same region.

## 5. Consolidation

59. **The constraint on stacking parts is the anatomical region, not the count**: ≤2 in the same region, cross-region stacking allowed;
    when one region has to carry three, the model makes up the difference by "drawing a separate individual" (a second animal appears in the image).
60. **Every round must carry a same-round, same-seed base control shot**, otherwise the objective differences are polluted by pose drift and
    one cannot tell "whether the transplant sentence took effect". This is the precondition for turning "objective metrics" into a usable criterion.
61. **Region diff is a one-way criterion**: region unchanged → the part definitely did not land; region changed → that does not prove the part caused it,
    so visual confirmation is required. Hence it is used only for **alerts** and does not enter the score.
