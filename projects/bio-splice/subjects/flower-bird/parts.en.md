# Flower-and-Bird Part Table (flower + bird)

> 🌐 Language: **English** | [中文](parts.md)

> Back to the [sub-theme home](README.en.md) ｜ [project home](../../README.en.md)

**Design source**: **"flower-and-bird" is the basic unit of Chinese painting**; the corolla and the bird's plumage mimic each other (a hummingbird like a flower, an orchid like a bee).
This sub-theme turns this "painting genre" into **one body**: bird feathers grow in the places of the flower's parts.

**Base ＝ large flower (magnolia / lily)**; donor ＝ three feather-type bird parts, **split by landing site**:

---

## 1. Part table and measured results (**only two landing sites work**)

| No. | Part | Landing site | Nature of landing site | Result |
|------|------|------|----------|------|
| P1 | Feather replacing petals | petal ring | **canonical structure** | ❌ **0%** (all three wordings wiped out, including the "covering style" that worked on cordyceps) |
| P5 | Down feather | flower centre | structure (stamens/pistil) | ⚠️ **partially works** (4.55 ×2): the stamens and pistil are read as downy |
| P6 | Plume feather | flower axis / flower stalk | **surface** (needs no bearing structure) | ✅ **fully works** (5.00 ×10) |

## 2. The petals verified "a canonical structure cannot be swapped out" once more

P1 was tried with three wordings, **0% on both the vacated version and the occupied version**:

| Wording | Result |
|------|------|
| `broad pale bird feathers in place of the petals` (relative clause) | ❌ |
| `a ring of broad pale bird feathers` (plain noun phrase) | ❌ |
| `broad pale bird feathers covering the flower` (covering style) | ❌ |

Yet **the very same "covering style" wording works on the cordyceps larva** (mycelium coating 4.55).
→ The difference is the landing site: **the insect's body surface is a "property", the flower's petal ring is a "structure"** (third verification of Rules 96/99).

## 3. Why the P6 plume feather is so strong

1. The landing site is the **flower axis (a surface)** —— a part growing out of a surface needs no bearing structure (Rule 97);
2. **Different in form + high recognizability**: a bundle of upright plume feathers and the flat-spread shape of petals are completely different (Rules 91/90);
3. Strong material contrast: white petals vs. semi-translucent feather filaments, which catch the light under backlight.

→ All 10 single-part images scored 5.00; this is the only part of this sub-theme that "works straight away".

## 4. The hit rate changes with the base (Rule 102)

Same wording (P5 + P6), the same presentation, **only the flower species changed from magnolia to lily**:

| Base | Hit rate |
|------|--------|
| Magnolia (R5/R6/R8) | 5/5 |
| **Lily (R7)** | **1/2** (took three extra seeds to make up 3 images, R9) |

→ **A new base must have its hit rate re-verified**; do not assume B is also stable just because "the wording was verified on A".
