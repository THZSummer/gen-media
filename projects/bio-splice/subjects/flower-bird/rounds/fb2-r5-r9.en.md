## 2. The supplementary round for Period 04: the hit rate drops with the flower species

R7 with the lily produced only one image (the other seed did not grow plume feathers), and R9 needed three more seeds to gather three images.
**The same wording, the same presentation, only the flower species changed** — therefore:

> **Rule 102: swapping the base requires re-validating the hit rate.**

## 3. Why this set looks good

**The plume feather and the petals are "different in form"** (a bundle of upright plume feathers vs. flat-spread petals),
and under backlight the feather filaments are semi-translucent — this is the key to why "flower-and-bird" works as a painting genre:
**not turning the flower into a bird, but letting a bird's feather grow out of the flower's "heart"**.

## 4. Takeaways

(For 102/103 see [fb2-r1-r4.md](fb2-r1-r4.en.md) and [parts.md](../parts.en.md).)

104. **"A feather growing from the flower's heart" is more artistic than "replacing petals with feathers"**:
     Mechanically, "canonical structures cannot be replaced" is a limitation, but **the limitation in turn yields a better composition** —
     after the landing site retreats from the "petal ring" to the "flower centre", the image looks more like "flower-and-bird" instead.
