## 2. This sub-theme overturned my own prediction

Before starting, I judged it **the hardest in Group B** by the "three questions": the Venus flytrap's morphology is highly set (Rule 92 unfavourable).
Measured: **all three worked** — because none of the **landing sites** (edge / leaf blade / cavity) is occupied by a canonical structure.

→ **Rule 99: the criterion lies in the "landing site", not the "whole".** (see [parts.md](../parts.en.md))

## 3. One shortcoming, recorded as it is

and when zoomed in it feels "pasted on". C is given only 4.
→ To remedy later: try phrasing with "eye socket + eyelid seam" (left for the next round or the next sub-theme to verify).
