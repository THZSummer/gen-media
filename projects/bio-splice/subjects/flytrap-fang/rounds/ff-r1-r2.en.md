## 2. Conclusion

**Boundary appendages (marginal teeth, marginal hairs, serrations) ≈ attributes, and can be freed** (a supplement to Rule 96):

| Type | Example | Whether freeing works |
|------|------|--------------|
| **Structure** (organs, limbs) | deer neck/deer legs, fish fins, cat paws, snake scales | ❌ the model restores them even when deleted (87/89) |
| **Attribute** (body-surface texture, colour, grain) | hyphae on the insect body (cordyceps) | ✅ works |
| **Boundary appendage** (marginal teeth, marginal hairs) | the Venus flytrap's marginal teeth (this sub-theme) | ✅ works |

## 3. Takeaways

(Rule 99: see [parts.md](../parts.en.md).)

100. **The applicability of "freeing a slot" is decided by "whether this spot is a structure or an attribute"**:
