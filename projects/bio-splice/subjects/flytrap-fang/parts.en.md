## 2. Rule 99: the criterion lies in the "landing site", not the "whole"

This sub-theme was originally predicted to be **the hardest in Group B** (the flytrap's morphological freedom is very low, Rule 92 unfavourable), yet **all three worked**.
The reason: **what matters is the freedom of "that one face at the landing site", not how set the whole organism is.**

| Part | Landing site | Nature of landing site | Result |
|------|------|----------|------|
| T1 mammal fangs | trap edge | boundary appendage (**property**) | ✅ fully works after vacating |
| T2 eye | leaf blade | empty face (a plant never had an eye) | ✅ works |
| T4 tongue | trap cavity | empty face (the cavity is empty) | ✅ fully works |
| — (control) deer neck/fish fin | neck/fin | **canonical structure** | ❌ form changes but not substance / 0% |

> **Rule 99: transplant difficulty depends on "whether the landing site is occupied by a canonical structure".**
> 92 (the base's morphological freedom) is the whole-organism view; 99 brings it down to the local level:
> **a set overall form does not hinder transplantation, as long as the landing site itself is empty or is merely an appendage property.**

## 3. The marginal teeth are a "property", not a "structure" (another face of Rule 96)

R1/R2 ran a control with the same seed, the same presentation and only the base wording changed:

| Base wording | Effect on T1 |
|----------|-----------|
| Occupied version: `a fringe of stiff marginal teeth` | the marginal teeth turn from fine long marginal hairs into **thick white tusks** (A=4) |
| **Vacated version**: do not describe the marginal teeth | the bare edge lines up a whole row of white tusks (**A=5**) |

→ **Boundary appendages (marginal teeth, marginal hairs, serrations) ≈ properties, and can be vacated**;
keep them distinct from "structures" (organs, limbs) —— this supplements 96.

## 4. An honest deduction for anatomical credibility

The eye in period 02 only reached 4.80 (C=4): **it is a bare eyeball with no eyelids and no eye socket**,
and zoomed in it feels "pasted on". → Recorded on the file:
**a single cross-kingdom part easily "looks pasted on"; adding a "socket / eyelid / seam" wording might save it (left as something to try later).**
