## 3. Takeaways

96. **What can be freed is "attributes"; what cannot be freed is "structure"**:
    body-surface texture, colour and grain can be deleted from the base description (freeing the placeholder works);
    organs and limbs cannot (Rules 89/87: deer legs, fish fins, cat paws, snake scales get restored even if deleted).
97. **A part that "grows out of the body surface" needs no extra bearing structure**: a stroma can grow as long as there is a "body surface" face,
    so it is far easier than "claws, which need limbs to grow"—**when choosing a part, first ask "what bearing surface does it need"**.
