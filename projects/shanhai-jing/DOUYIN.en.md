## 0. Verdict at a Glance

| Question | Conclusion | Confidence |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| Can we publish | **Yes.** Only three hard gates: real-name verification, an **explicit AI-content label** (now a mandatory publishing step), and never claiming "restoration/authentic artifact" or turning quoted efficacy lines into promises | High (ministry document + platform announcement) |
| Is the theme a fit | **The subject matter fits well** (Shan Hai Jing + AI already has a 3.7-million-like precedent); **but the current "1080x1440 finished plates" are not a native Douyin format** — posting them as-is buys nothing beyond baseline reach | High (documented cases + format analysis) |
| Can it pull substantial traffic | **Yes, but not on that path.** Substantial traffic lives in **narrative short video** and **long-form illustrated essays**; static image posts are only for filling the schedule and probing | Medium (ceiling is documented, the median is not) |

One line: **the subject is a gold mine; the format has to be rebuilt.**
Our real moat (verifiable sourcing, correct counts, seal handling) is not the selling point on Douyin — it is the **hook inside a video script**, and that determines how we rewrite.

> **Phasing decision (settled 2026-10-07)**: **short term publishes image posts with in-app music only; video moves to the long term.**
> The three traffic tiers below and the route analysis in §5 are unchanged; only the **execution order** changes. Image posts first, to learn which creature names carry traffic and which cover earns clicks; the video phase then starts from that data. The image-post phase therefore chases data, not reach.

## 1. Three Hard Things to Do First

### 1.1 Account

- Register a personal account + **real-name verification**. You can publish without it, but verification is required to livestream, earn revenue, sell goods and take Xingtu orders (monetisation thresholds in §6).
- One device, one account; do not switch accounts on one device (matrix risk control).
- **Do not trust "account warming" folklore**: the circulating "7-day warm-up, like-only for the first 3 days" has no platform statement behind it and originates from marketing blog posts ([low confidence](https://insight.xiaoduoai.com/latest-news/https-insight-xiaoduoai-com-service-based-marketing-the-traffic-pool-of-new-accounts-is-30-higher-than-that-of-old-ones-only-like-without-posting-in-the-first-3-days-of-nurturing-an-account-a-complete.html)). What actually drives distribution is content quality and vertical consistency.

### 1.2 AI Labelling: Mandatory, and a Required Publishing Step

- Regulation: the four-department *Measures for Labelling AI-Generated Synthetic Content*, **in force 2025-09-01**, requiring **explicit + implicit labels** ([Guangming Daily](https://m.gmw.cn/2025-03/14/content_1303991453.htm), [Hebei High Court reprint](https://www.hebeicourt.gov.cn/article/detail/2025/03/id/8754555.shtml)).
- Douyin published its *Announcement on Upgrading the AI Content Labelling Feature* the same day ([Bianews reprint](https://bianews.com/news/details?id=220066)):
  - **We label proactively**: publishing screen → Advanced Settings → Assistant Declaration → **"Content generated by AI"**. It covers **video / image / text / audio** — image posts and long-form essays included.
  - **If we do not**: the platform detects suspected AIGC and adds "suspected AI generation, please verify" itself; if it verifies an implicit metadata label it adds "contains AI-generated content". **Not labelling does not make a piece look hand-drawn — it only lets someone else label it for us, which reads worse.**
  - The platform **writes** an implicit label (including distribution attributes) into the file. Do not use our metadata-stripping capability afterwards to wash out the platform's label — that is clear circumvention.
- In 2026-05 the Cyberspace Administration of China made labelling a **mandatory step for every short video**, with 6 required label types (including "contains AI-generated content" and "contains fictional performance"), plus retrospective labelling of existing content; since January it cleaned up 520,000+ violating short videos and penalised 68,000+ accounts ([CCTV via China Youth Daily](http://news.cyol.com/gb/articles/2026-05/12/content_xaoxZGFVxO.html)).
- **Our action**: put "tick the AI declaration" into the publishing S.O.P. (§2) and do it every period; state in the copy that it is "AI-generated, created from the *Shan Hai Jing* text and Guo Pu's commentary", turning compliance into credibility.

### 1.3 Copyright and Content Red Lines (only what concerns us)

| Item | Our current state | What to do |
|------|-------------------|-----------|
| Classical text | Public domain (Guo Pu edition, checked character by character) | Cite the source edition (Shidian Guji SK2098) |
| Typesetting font | Noto Serif CJK (OFL, commercially usable) | If a "real seal layer" later uses a third-party seal script font, **confirm commercial licensing first** (that layer is not implemented yet) |
| BGM | Use the in-app music library | In-app use by personal accounts is handled by the platform; **selling or commercial commissions require commercially licensed tracks** |
| Seal | Square red seal, real characters composited by tooling | Do not make round seals with a five-pointed star or agency names (easily mistaken for a forged official seal); never let the model draw a seal (it draws garbled ones — see §3) |
| Quoted efficacy lines | e.g. "wearing it removes fear" | Quote only; never rewrite into an efficacy promise (warding off evil, curing illness) — that touches superstition and false advertising |
| Positioning | Derivative artwork, not an artifact | Never write "restoration / unearthed / authentic / high-resolution scan" |
| Horror level | Later entries include Xiang-Liu and Tao-Tie types | Avoid dark and gory imagery — the platform explicitly lists "excessive darkness, horror and the bizarre" as throttling territory ([Tencent Cloud community digest](https://developer.cloud.tencent.cn/article/2667206), self-media source) |

## 2. Publishing Formats and Asset Specs

### 2.1 How to Choose Among the Four Publishing Entries (the list the publishing screen offers)

| Publishing entry | Format the platform gives | How we use it | Frequency | Expectation |
|------------------|---------------------------|---------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Publish video** | Short video (BGM / subtitles / cover) | ✅ **Main line**: 15–45 s process video | 1–2 per week | Highest ceiling (the only entry that can plausibly pass 100K) |
| **Publish image post** | Multi-image swipe + BGM (≥2 images, 4–6 recommended; upper limit per the publishing screen) | ✅ **Floor + probe**: reuse existing plates directly | 2–3 per week | Baseline reach; sets the tag and tests which creature name carries traffic |
| **Publish article** | Long illustrated essay: ≤8000 characters / ≤30 images / title + summary + cover, surfacing in the feed as an auto-scrolling, music-backed video-like card | ✅ **Secondary priority**: rewrite review records into general-audience sourcing essays | 1 per creature | Long tail + saves; the platform offers dedicated support and 1-to-1 guidance |
| **Publish panoramic video** | 360° panorama (equirectangular projection, the viewer rocks the phone to change angle) | ⛔ **Skip it** | — | Our assets are flat plates; they cannot produce valid panoramic material |

**Why this ordering**

- **Video is the main battlefield**: Douyin's recommendation scores completion, dwell time and interaction, all of which static images are weak at; the cases also show that this subject only breaks out on video (the [3.7M-like AI short film](https://vv.lmtw.com/mzw/content/detail/id/256150)).
- **How to fit our 3:4 plates into video**: do not drop 1080x1440 straight into the video entry (ugly letterboxing). Build a **1080x1920 canvas** — plate centred at 100% or 90% width, top and bottom filled with a **blurred enlargement of the same plate**, cartouche/subtitles over the empty bands, first frame as the cover. This preserves the plate's composition while filling the screen.
- **Image posts are not filler**: they are the best container for multi-image comparison — magnified count crops, seal before/after, same-round controls — which is exactly the material only we have; but **do not post the `controls/` set raw**; pair each with one line saying what it proves.
- **Articles (long essays) are our cheap exclusive**: `rounds/*.md` already holds plenty of "why this creature is hard to draw / how we verified it", and 1500–3000 characters is a good essay — there is no need to fill 8000; the platform is giving long essays **dedicated traffic support** ([Science and Technology Daily](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2025-12/26/content_453850.html), [PChome](https://article.pchome.net/news/8873.html)). Note: currently **PC Creator Centre only** (mobile rolling out), and whether an account already has access depends on the screen (medium confidence).
- **Why not panoramic video**: it needs 360° material (equirectangular projection), while our output is flat. Forcing it would neither meet the material requirement nor look good. If we later build a genuine "orbiting beast" 3D/video piece (we already have image-to-video techniques), that goes through **Publish video**, not the panoramic entry.

### 2.2 Specs and Rework per Entry

| Format | Douyin spec | What we already have | Rework |
|--------|-------------|----------------------|--------|
| **Image post** | Multi-image swipe + BGM; 4–6 images recommended (upper limit per the live publishing screen) | 3 finished plates per creature (1080x1440, 3:4), usable as-is | **None** (montage + copy) |
| **Short video** | 1080x1920 (full screen) or 1080x1440; 15–45 s; cover + BGM + subtitles | `period-01/` finals + `controls/` comparison images + `rounds/rNN-review.md` process notes | **Medium** (editing needed; material is ready) |
| **Long illustrated essay** | Launched 2025-12: up to **8000 characters** + up to 30 images + BGM; entry is **Creator Centre on PC → HD Publish → Publish Article** (mobile rolling out); appears in the feed in a video-like form, and **quality articles get dedicated traffic support** ([Science and Technology Daily](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2025-12/26/content_453850.html)) | `rounds/*.md` sourcing and review records | **Large** (must be rewritten for a general audience) |

### 2.3 Publishing S.O.P. (tick as you go)

1. Assets: decide the first image/cover first (it drives ~80% of click-through) → 3–9 images or the finished cut → consistent aspect ratio, already stripped via `ffkit strip`.
2. BGM: pick from the in-app library; keep it around -18 dB so it never buries the voice.
3. Copy: the first sentence is the hook (see §3); **the second sentence must contain "AI" and "*Shan Hai Jing*"**; end with a question to pull comments.
4. Hashtags: `#shanhaijing` `#mythicalbeasts` `#AIart` `#guofeng` plus 1–2 topical tags (do not stack ten).
5. **Advanced Settings → Assistant Declaration → Content generated by AI (mandatory tick).**
6. Channel: mobile app (image posts / short video) or the PC Creator Centre (long essays).
7. Reply within 30 minutes of publishing (provoke the "are the counts right" argument; interaction rate is one of the three quality-score components).
8. Archive: record the actual title, cover and numbers back into `subjects/<slug>/` as the basis for the next round.

## 3. Theme Fit Score

Scale 1–5, 5 best. **"Fit" means "it moves in Douyin's distribution environment", not content quality.**

| Dimension | Score | Note |
|-----------|-------|------|
| National recognition of the subject | **5** | The *Shan Hai Jing* is a compulsory-education cultural symbol; the nine-tailed fox and Tao-Tie are common knowledge ([Guduor / LMTW](https://vv.lmtw.com/mzw/content/detail/id/256150)) |
| Proven hit potential | **5** | A-Yan's *Crossing the Shan Hai Jing* drew 3.7M+ Douyin likes and ~600K new followers in a week; AI Madhouse's "AI-restored Shan Hai Jing" topic passed 100M plays; in 2025 AI creators grew 191% and AI creators above 10K followers grew 160% (Newrank report on Douyin's first AI creation contest) |
| Format nativeness | **2** | The biggest gap: the hit format is a "narrative short film / familiar gods leading a dungeon run", not "static plate + sourcing notes". Douyin's unit of consumption is **emotion and story**, not aesthetics and knowledge |
| Differentiation | **4** | Among a flood of saturated AI beasts, "correct counts + verifiable text + real characters composited by tooling" is genuinely scarce; but the difference **must be translated into a hook** before anyone watches (§5 examples) |
| Information gain | **4** | This maps to the 35% component of the quality score — sourcing is our natural advantage (quality-score detail in §4) |
| Sustainable cadence | **5** | 30,000+ characters, 400+ creatures; a natural series structure; 3 creatures / 9 finished plates plus control sets already reusable |
| Visual impact | **3** | The current idiom is **ochre-coloured gongbi** (stronger than pure line drawing, README §5), but it is still in the "quiet" band in a feed; the cover title has to compensate |
| Compliance risk | **3** | AI labelling is a duty rather than a risk; the real risks are seal styling, quoted efficacy lines and horror-adjacent subjects (§1.3) |
| Monetisation headroom | **3** | Cultural accounts do not scale through selling goods; the paths are Xingtu tasks + long-essay traffic + IP licensing/publishing, and they are slow (§6) |

**Verdict: subject 5, format 2 → "a fit, but it must be rewritten for Douyin".**
Moving the Xiaohongshu package (illustrated plate + long sourcing copy) over unchanged means fighting Douyin's strongest arena with its weakest format.

## 4. Traffic Expectations (Tiered, With Evidence)

### 4.1 Platform Baseline Numbers

| Number | Source | Confidence |
|--------|--------|------------|
| New accounts get cold-start support for the first 5–10 posts, with an initial pool of roughly 500–1000 plays (300–800 for older accounts) | Marketing blog, no platform statement | **Low** (order of magnitude only) |
| Douyin and Toutiao launched a **content quality score**: originality 40% / information gain 35% / interaction rate 25%, with **below 60 meaning throttling and natural traffic cut off**; on day one 120,000+ low-quality AI items were flagged and affected accounts lost about 60% of traffic on average | [Sohu self-media analysis](https://www.sohu.com/a/1034712070_122616458) (single source, no official page found) | **Medium-low** (direction credible, figures unconfirmed) |
| Douyin MAU 907M (2025-10, QuestMobile) | [iHeima](http://www.iheima.com/article-394394.html) | Medium |
| AI content **is not itself a violation**; what gets penalised is "false, infringing, low-taste traffic bait" | Douyin official FAQ | High |

**Two operational conclusions follow**:
(1) Our sourcing content has a **tailwind** on originality + information gain, and a **headwind** on interaction rate (static images and long text interact poorly by nature);
(2) **Mass-posting the same composition with a new skin is judged low quality** — we publish one creature per period, which avoids this naturally, but **do not post the `controls/` comparison set as content**; those are material, not works.

### 4.2 Three Tiers (First Month; assuming 2–3 posts a week, vertical-only, every post hooked)

| Tier | Premise | Plays | Followers | My probability estimate |
|------|---------|-------|-----------|------------------------|
| Conservative | Static image posts only, no hook, no editing | 500–3000 per post; 10K–30K per month | 100–500 | High |
| Mid | Image posts + 15–45 s process videos (narrative), sustained 4 weeks | 3000–50,000 per post; **at least one above 100K** | 1000–5000 | Medium |
| Ceiling | Narrative shorts + trending BGM/memes + official AI programmes/contests | 100K–1M+ plays per post, hit posts at a million likes | 10K–100K | Low (subject, luck and effort must align) |

> These are **order-of-magnitude inferences**: the ceiling is supported by a public case (3.7M likes), the median has no public data at all, and I have no access to Douyin's backend. **Do not treat them as promises** — their purpose is prioritisation, not prediction.
> What can be fixed in advance is the **ordering**: video > long essay > static image post; hooked > unhooked; series > one-offs.

## 5. Three Routes and a 30-Day Cadence

### Route A: Static image posts (reuse existing assets, zero rework)

- How: 3 plates per creature → first image is the illustrated plate plus a title; body text is the original passage + plain-language translation + a note on the count; tick the AI declaration.
- Value: **calibrates the account tag**, tests which creature name carries traffic on its own (Bo-Yi versus the nine-tailed fox is an order of magnitude apart), near-zero cost.
- Expectation: the conservative tier. **Do not treat it as the main line.**

### Route B: Process short video (the main line, highest ceiling)

- 15–45 s, vertical 1080x1920; a fixed structure of **hook → conflict → reversal → finished plate → question**:

| Seconds | Visual | Line / subtitle (example: Bo-Yi) |
|---------|--------|----------------------------------|
| 0–3 | Open on a close-up of a "nine-tailed sheep" plate | "In the *Shan Hai Jing* there is a sheep with **nine tails, four ears, and eyes on its back**." |
| 3–10 | Quick cuts of three failures (we already have `controls/bo-yi-zimage-v1-101-single-tail.png`) | "I asked AI to draw it — **21 images, 0 correct**." |
| 10–22 | One line of source text → switch engine → works first try | "The problem was not the prompt. It had **never seen this creature**." |
| 22–32 | Final plate + zoomed count (corner sheets from `corner_sheet.py`) | "One engine change and it is right first try. **Nine tails. Not one more, not one less.**" |
| 32–40 | Seal comparison (garbled seal → erased, 7024 px difference) | "It also stamped a **garbled seal** of its own — we erased it." |
| 40–45 | Question | "Next up: Di-Jiang, six legs, four wings, no face. What should it look like?" |

- We **already have all the material**: 3 finished plates per creature, `controls/` comparisons, failure rounds in `rounds/rNN-review.md`, seal-erasure pixel evidence, corner zoom sheets. Only editing and voice-over are missing.
- Expectation: starts at the mid tier; a hit reaches the ceiling tier.

### Route C: Long sourcing essay (platform support, built for saves)

- How: rewrite `rounds/r01-review.md` into a 1500–3000 character general-audience essay (**not** a raw review log): how the creature is actually described in the source → how Guo Pu annotated it → how later ages drew it → why AI gets it wrong → how we verified it.
- Payoff: image and long-form content weight **saves** heavily, and Douyin is giving long essays **dedicated traffic support** (launched 2025-12, see §2).
- Expectation: individual pieces will not go viral, but the **long tail and follower quality** are the best of the three, and it is the only low-cost channel that monetises our most expensive capability (sourcing).

### Phased Cadence (short-term image posts / long-term video)

**Short term (weeks 1–4: image posts with in-app music only)**

| Week | Action | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| Week 1 | Real-name verification → run the full §2.3 flow once (including the AI declaration) → publish 2–3 posts: nine-tailed fox / lu-shu / bo-yi | Set the tag, validate the publishing chain, confirm the label shows |
| Weeks 2–3 | 3 posts a week, with **cover A/B tests** (full plate versus cartouche plate; hooked title versus plain) | Learn which cover earns clicks |
| Week 4 | 1 long essay per creature (Route C) + review: save rate, comment quality, differences between creature names | Distil which creature names carry traffic; write it back into `subjects/<slug>/publish.json` |
| Throughout | Tick the AI declaration on every post; follow official AI programmes (AI Creation Wave Plan / the first AI Creation Contest: 4M CNY cash + 20M Jimeng credits + traffic support + IP development) | Compliance plus the cheapest traffic entry |

**Long term (from week 5: video)** — start condition: at least 12 image posts published, with two datasets in hand (best cover scheme, creature-name traffic).

| Order | Action | Note |
|-------|--------|------|
| (1) | **Deterministic edited video** (main line): `01-<slug>.png` plate + subtitles + slow push-in (ffmpeg `zoompan`) + in-app BGM | Controllable and reproducible, zero local cost; needs a `short_video.py` with self-checks |
| (2) | **Generative video** (accent): 3–5 s opening or atmosphere shots via `image-to-video-fastvideo3` | It breaks countable traits (lost tails, blurred ears), so it requires same-round controls, scoring, and a separate video rubric |
| (3) | Bring the video skills into the gate: add both skills' offline self-checks and real-machine `--check` to `check.sh` step 3 (both free) | Turns "we can do video" into a pipeline fact |

## 6. Monetisation Thresholds (Xingtu, updated 2026-09)

Source: [Julian Xingtu "New Creator Onboarding Handbook"](https://www.xingtu.cn/help-center/author/109194) (official).

| Task type | Activation threshold |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Onboarding to Xingtu | **No follower requirement** (compliant content tone is enough) |
| Douyin live submission task | Followers ≥1000 and **valid followers ≥200**, plus ≥3 livestreams in the last 14 days, each ≥25 min |
| Xingchuan task | 1000 ≤ followers ≤ 1M, valid followers ≥500 |
| Recruitment task · fixed price | 10K ≤ followers ≤ 5M, valid followers ≥500 |
| Xingguang co-investment | Followers ≥10K, valid followers ≥500 |
| Image-post task | Exists as its own task type ([image-post fixed-price settlement](https://www.xingtu.cn/help-center/author/121696)); thresholds follow the task page |
| Product sharing / selling | Real-name verification + platform follower threshold + deposit ([Douyin E-commerce Learning Centre](https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/wap/article/aHXZ3Jpj3Fxf)) |

**How to read it**: **1000 valid followers** is the first real monetisation step. At the §4 cadence that is reachable in 1–2 months under the mid expectation. Commission per deal for cultural accounts is not high, so do not make monetisation the first goal — build vertical consistency and a series IP first.

## 7. Risks and Countermeasures

| Risk | Trigger | Countermeasure |
|------|---------|----------------|
| Judged low-quality AI content → throttled | Same composition with a new skin, images with no information, broken rendering | One creature per period, each with sourcing gain; comparison images stay material; pass the existing A–F scoring before publishing |
| Unlabelled → platform adds "suspected" | Missing the declaration | Written into S.O.P. step 5; self-check the post page for the label after publishing |
| Seal misread | Round / five-pointed-star / agency styling | Square or free-form private seals only; never official-seal styling; better to omit than to fake until the real seal layer lands |
| Efficacy / superstition risk | "Wearing it removes fear" read as a promise | Always mark as a quoted line; never write "wards off evil / cures illness / brings safety" |
| Artifact misrepresentation | Copy reads "restoration / authentic" | Fixed wording: AI-generated derivative artwork based on the source text and commentary |
| Horror adjacency | Xiang-Liu, Tao-Tie subjects | Decide the tone as "strange", not "dreadful", before generating; no blood, no dark filters |
| Infringement | Non-commercial fonts / tracks / third-party images | OFL-commercially usable fonts only; in-app or licensed BGM; never use someone else's AI images for comparison — use our own `controls/` |
| Cadence collapse | One video costs too much to edit | Route A guarantees a floor; Route B needs only 1–2 per week |
