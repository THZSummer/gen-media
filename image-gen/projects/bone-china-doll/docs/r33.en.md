> User feedback on R32: **"she has no princess temperament, a bit old-looking"**.

## Diagnosis: R28's cut removed two things

R30 found only one of them (the glaze defect list). This round audits the base prompt word by word; the second is **the whole block of facial description**:

| Face/temperament keyword | `BASE_Q10` (phase-2 authority) | R32 base prompt |
|------------------|---------------------------|----------|
| `elegant` / `adult` / `young` | ✅ | **0 occurrences** |
| `eyebrow` / `eyelash` / `eye` / `lip` / `nose` / `cheek` | ✅ all present | **0 occurrences each** |
| `blush` | `the faintest soft blush` | **0 occurrences** |

`BASE_Q10` originally had a whole sentence:

> An **elegant** princess face with refined **adult** proportions: **oval face, high cheekbones,
> straight nose bridge**, serene closed lips, **almond eyes** of realistic size with a subtle crease,
> individually **painted eyelashes**, **eyebrows** painted hair by hair, **the faintest soft blush**.

The base prompt has not a single facial-feature word → the model can only improvise a face from the generic word `princess`,
producing a **mature, bland, bloodless** generic adult female face.

**Aggravating factor**: the negative bans `blush makeup / rouge / blush patches / 腮红` (added by R27 to cure the "deliberate blush"),
while the positive has no blood-colour description at all → a bloodless face, the direct source of the "old look".

## Changes this round (one group of variables: restore "face + bearing")

1. **Restore the whole facial definition above**, and add an explicit age anchor: `a young woman, refined and fresh-faced, never matronly and never severe`
2. **Add a bearing sentence to the Chinese layer**: `虽在闲居，气度仍在：肩背挺秀，下颌微收，目光清亮，一份矜贵自持`;
   to `神态慵懒闲适，不拘礼数` add `，却自带一份矜贵` at the end
3. **Remove the "blanket blush ban" from the negative** (`blush makeup / rouge / blush patches / 腮红`),
   keeping only `circular / doll-like / heavy blush` and the `applied cosmetics / 浓妆 / 影楼` class of bans
   → when the positive wants "an extremely faint blood colour", the negative must not ban it at the same time

Base prompt 1282 → 1664 characters. All three share R32's seed / canvas / material sentence **word for word** (`assert` locks it down).

### Two deliberately handled contradictions

- **Age must be anchored on both sides**: historically the project pushed the face toward adulthood to cure an "infantile face shape" (R2–R4),
  and this feedback points to "too old". So the age anchor is written as "early twenties + never matronly",
  rather than a vague "adult".
- **Languid ≠ lacking bearing**: phase-5's brief is a "languid everyday feel", while this round wants "princess bearing".
  The two do not conflict — what is added is "still bearing even at leisure" (shoulders and back / jaw / gaze), **without touching the languid setting**,
  and stiff posing stays banned (`stiff formal pose / studio pose / 端坐 / 摆拍`).

## Key conclusions

1. **"No temperament" is a lack of description, not the model being incapable**: after restoring phase-2's facial-feature sentence,
   eyelashes, eyebrows, eye shape and lip shape **each take effect** — consistent with the reliability of **named parts** like
   `painted eyelashes / eyebrows painted hair by hair` (echoing R19: named objects are reliable).
2. **Keyword "hits" can be verified item by item**: under the same-seed A/B, R33's improvements over R32 map one-to-one to the clauses
   (eyelashes ← painted eyelashes; brows ← painted hair by hair; lip and cheek blood colour ← removing the blush ban + faintest soft blush).
3. **`adult` is a dangerous word**: it was introduced in R4/R5 to cure an "infantile face shape" and stayed in the facial sentence ever since.
   In a tight portrait it is executed literally as **wrinkles and nasolabial folds** — **a word introduced to cure one fault becomes a new fault in another context**.
4. **The age anchor's position is too weak**: R33's `a young woman ... never matronly` is written at the **end** of the facial sentence,
   in the middle of the whole prompt. By the project's own "position is weight" (verified four times in R23/R24, R27, R28),
   this position has almost no weight. → Directly gives rise to [R34](r34.en.md): move the age anchor **up front** into the subject sentence,
   change `adult` to `youthful`, and add anti-aging words to the negative.
