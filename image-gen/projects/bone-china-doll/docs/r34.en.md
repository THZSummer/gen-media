## Key conclusions

1. **The age anchor's position determines whether it takes effect** ("position is weight", 5th verification): the same meaning,
   placed at the end of the facial sentence (R33) is almost ineffective; placed in the subject (R34) it takes effect immediately.
2. **The negative is the right place to exclude aging features**: after `wrinkles / 法令纹 / 眼袋 / 老气` were put into the negative,
   the fine lines in the tight portrait decreased markedly. Writing "no wrinkles" in the positive is unreliable.
3. ~~**"Porcelain-surface defects" and the "old look" are two ends of the same lever**~~ ← **this item has been overturned by the R25 audit before R35; see the correction below**.
   The inference at the time was: the `polishing marks / hairline drag lines / orange-peel ripple` written in R30–R32
   were executed on the skin as wrinkles, and were therefore one source of the "old look". **That inference is wrong.**

   ### ⚠️ After-the-fact correction (before R35, from auditing R25)

   Auditing phase-4's material highland R25 (`out/r25/bc-r25-skin-square`) overturned this item:

   | | R25 (reads as **young + hard glaze**) | R30–R34 |
   |---|---|---|
   | Defect list (5 items) | **Word-for-word identical, all present** | Same |
   | Facial description | **None** (eyebrow/eyelash/almond all 0) | Present from R33 |
   | Age words | **None** (`adult` / `young` 0 occurrences each) | R33 has `adult`; R34 changed it to `youthful` |

   R25's final is a face that is **in her early twenties, with hard glaze sheen on the forehead, a glaze pull-back pinhole on the cheek and a faint blush on the cheeks** —
   **the defect list and a "young glaze surface" are fully compatible**.

   So the real causality is:
   - **The culprit for the old look is `adult` in `refined adult proportions`** — a word I recovered from `BASE_Q10`
     in R33, originally introduced in R4/R5 to cure an "infantile face shape" and **never re-reviewed**.
   - **The porcelain feel receding in R34 is most likely the anti-aging negatives** (`aged skin / mature face / wrinkles / fine lines`)
     suppressing the glaze's surface variation along with them, **rather than the defect list at work**.
   → R35 therefore changes to **removing only the anti-aging negatives** (single variable), keeping the complete defect list and the positive young anchor.
4. **The complete R30→R34 trajectory** (for decision-making):
   | Round | Skin reads as | Pros | Cons |
   |----|----------|------|------|
   | R29 | Warm-toned matte real-human skin | Natural, with living blood colour | No porcelain feel |
   | R31 | Real-human skin + satin sheen | Porcelain feel first appears | Bland features, old-looking |
   | R32 | Soft overall glaze sheen | The balance point of half real, half bone china | Features still bland and old-looking |
   | R33 | Porcelain feel + refined features | Lashes/brows/eyes/lips each in place, with blood colour | Fine lines appear → old-looking |
   | **R34** | **Dewy young skin + porcelain sheen** | **Young, refined, good complexion** | **The porcelain-surface defects are suppressed; the porcelain feel recedes slightly** |
