**New issues**:
1. "large softbox key light" still **renders the fixture literally** → remove fixture words entirely and describe only the direction and quality of the light
2. "subsurface scattering" triggered **facial patch artifacts** in image 2 → change to a safer translucency description (thin porcelain translucency at the ear rims/fingertips)
3. Too many pearl strands caused **floating breakage** → narrow to "a single strand of pearls around the neckline"
4. The face shape is still toddlerish → needs a stronger proportion constraint (adult princess, jawline, head-to-body ratio)

## Changes for the next round (R4)

1. Lighting: delete all fixture nouns, change to **soft directional light from upper left + gentle rim light, no equipment of any kind in the background**
2. Translucency: change to **thin translucent porcelain edge on the ears and fingertips, warm glow through the rim** (avoid flat patch artifacts)
3. Pearls: **single strand of pearls at the neckline** (multiple dangling strands forbidden)
4. Proportions: **elegant adult princess proportions, defined jawline, tapered neck, small head, not a child**
5. Keep the five-camera strategy, change the seed block (400+)
