**Still improvable**:
1. The face is **too flawless** and has a CGI smoothness — porcelain is supposed to be fine-grained, but it needs micro glaze relief and colour variation
2. The "macro" of `hands-embroidery` was not executed by the model (the framing words need a stronger crop description)
3. The light ratio can be more extreme (a stronger light-shadow boundary) to enhance the sculptural feel

## Changes for the next round (R9)

1. Add **micro glaze** requirements: `microscopic glaze texture / polishing marks / microscopic bubbles in the glaze / matte unglazed foot rim`
2. Make the **anti-CGI** instruction explicit: `absolutely no CGI smoothness` + `focus stacked, ultra high resolution scan quality, natural sensor grain`
3. Framing: use "fills the frame / cropped just below the collarbone" to strongly constrain the macro crop
4. steps → 30, cfg → 3.5
