3. 光比可以更极端（更强的明暗交界线）以增强雕塑感

## 下一轮改法（R9）

1. 追加**微观釉面**要求：`microscopic glaze texture / polishing marks / microscopic bubbles in the glaze / matte unglazed foot rim`
2. 明确 **anti-CGI**：`absolutely no CGI smoothness` + `focus stacked, ultra high resolution scan quality, natural sensor grain`
3. 构图：用"fills the frame / cropped just below the collarbone"强约束微距裁切
4. steps → 30，cfg → 3.5
