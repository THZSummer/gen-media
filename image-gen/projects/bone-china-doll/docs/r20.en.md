## Key conclusions

1. **The cropping method works under the Eastern theme too**, and **needs no new prompt wording** — simply reuse the R17/R18 base images.
   This confirms once again: **macro is a geometric problem, not a semantic one.**
2. **"Scene objects" are less reliable than "named jewellery"**:
   - High reliability: `璎珞 / 步摇 / 云肩 / 金镶玉带 / 瓷鞋` (object type)
   - Medium reliability: `团扇 / 屏风 / 木几` (worked in R17 and R18, failed in R20 → unstable)
   - Low reliability: `花钿` (a facial makeup decoration, failed all three times)
   > Rule: **the closer to "a physical object that can take shape on its own", the more reliable; the closer to "makeup/framing", the less reliable.**
