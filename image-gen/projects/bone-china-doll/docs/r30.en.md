> Cause: comparing `out/final-set-life/` and `out/final-set-gauze/` side by side —
> the phase-5 batch reads as "**a real person in hanfu having her photo taken**"; the bone china texture is gone.

## Diagnosis (not an aesthetic drift, but a mechanical cause)

Comparing the two stages' base prompts word by word, the difference is not the level of the wording but that **a list was deleted**:

| Base prompt | Length | Named glaze defects |
|------|------|--------------|
| `BASE_H4_EN` (phase-4 material layer) | 844 characters | **All eight items present** + `never a uniform flat surface` + `absolutely no CGI smoothness and no plastic` |
| `BASE_H8_POSE` (phase-5 short base prompt) | 776 characters | **Only the generic phrase `fine micro-texture in the glaze` remains** |

To get the full-body pose, R28 cut the base prompt from 1802 to 635 — **that cut also removed the named defect list**.
**This is the same mechanism as R11's degeneration** (deleting material vocabulary so that composition dominates); this time, however, it hid inside
the "short base prompt", a change proven effective at the time, so it drew no attention then.

## Changes this round (single variable)

`BASE_H9_POSE` = `BASE_H8_POSE` with the material sentence swapped for phase-4's defect list, **nothing else touched**:

- The **pose text of shot 1 `propped-bolster` and shot 2 `mat-by-window` is carried over word for word from R29**
- `assert` guarantees `BASE_H9_POSE == BASE_H8_POSE.replace(材质句)`,
  and asserts that the archived prompts of R29/R30 are unchanged
- **Deliberately does not restore** `a soft blush of warm colour across the cheeks` from `BASE_H4_EN` —
  R27 already proved that sentence (when placed up front) is the source of the "deliberate blush"

Base prompt 776 → 1178 characters; whole prompt 852 → 1254 characters.
**Note this magnitude**: with an 1802-character base prompt R27 got 0/3 full bodies — R30 comes right up against that dangerous length,
so this round is also a stress test of "**can pose-up-front withstand a long base prompt**".

## Key conclusions

1. **Material density does not pull the camera in** (this round's cleanest conclusion): raising the material sentences from 776 to 1178 characters
   (1254 total, approaching the 1283 at which R27 failed), all three **still hold the full-body composition**.
   → Consistent with the inference of R21/R29: **in "a long list is the enemy of composition", the enemy is the "exhaustive clothing list", not "total length", and certainly not "mentioning material"**.
   Pose up front is still an effective ordering device.
2. **Material vocabulary really is the switch for the "bone china feel"**, but R30 only recovered half of it:
   same-region crops show R29's hand is **matte real-human skin** and R30's hand has **glaze sheen** — the porcelain feel really did come back.
3. **The named defect list was not executed**: `pinholes` / `orange-peel` / `polishing marks` / `kiln grit`
   are all invisible in the image, and what is gained instead is **a more uniform surface** — exactly the
   `never a uniform flat surface` that the prompt explicitly forbids. This is the same class of phenomenon as R9's "microscopic glaze was not executed".
4. **A key subordinate clause is missing**: `BASE_H9_POSE` lacks `with crisp specular highlights where the light strikes`
   (which `BASE_H4_EN` has). This sentence is very likely the real switch for "the glaze being seen", not merely the defect list.
   → Directly gives rise to [R31](r31.en.md) (restore this sentence + use a tight-portrait canvas as the audit sheet).
5. **Judging "fineness" needs enough pixels**: R30's square canvas is a **full body**, the face only about 180 px,
   and when magnified the detail is smoothed away by interpolation — **the framing of the audit sheet matters as much as the canvas**. R25 could reach conclusions back then
   because it used a 2048² **tight portrait**. This is recorded as this round's lesson.
