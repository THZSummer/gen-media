## Core methodological correction this round: **Chinese-English division of labour**

R22 proved the strengths of each language, so R23 **mixes them in layers**:

| Layer | Language | Responsible for | Basis |
|---|------|------|------|
| **Character / expression / jewellery / clothing / environment** | **Chinese** | "**what there is**" | The R22 Chinese passage hit the Boshan censer's blue smoke, the screen's pictorial feel, the gold yingluo, the jade ring and the gold-woven collar in one try — noticeably more precise than English |
| **Material (skin)** | **English** | "**what it is made of**" | the glaze / translucency / microscopic-texture vocabulary of `BASE_Q10` has 21 rounds of validation |

The key: the English material passage's **subject is `her skin`**, not `a doll` — **a living person + bone china material, the two are not contradictory**.

Two reverse constraints were also added to cure R22's "photo-studio feel":
- Positive: `妆极淡，近乎素面`, `眉如远山，不施浓黛`, `唇上只点一点浅胭脂`
- Negative: `heavy makeup / bright red lipstick / cosplay / studio glamour shot / 浓妆 / 影楼 / 网红妆`

## Key conclusions

1. **The Chinese-English division of labour works**, and it solved R22's makeup problem (the "bare face" constraint took effect).
2. **But the English material passage was diluted**: it sits after a ~450-character Chinese passage and has almost no weight in the prompt.
   R11 (cropping up front) and R15 (canvas) already proved that **what comes first in the prompt wins**.
3. **The skin close-up crop (`out/macro-skin/skin-face-r23.png`) is the decisive evidence**:
   the eyes are excellent (radial iris texture, double catchlight, upper and lower eyelashes each distinct),
   but the skin **has no glaze sheen, no translucency, no microscopic porcelain-body texture** → the user's "bone china texture" did not meet the bar.

## Changes for the next round (R24)

**Change only one thing**: move the English material passage **to the very front of the prompt** (cfg / steps / size / seed strategy all untouched,
keeping it single-variable). Two additional changes in the same direction:
- The Chinese passage gains a **porcelain-body layer** (glaze / translucency where the body is thin / bubbles under the glaze, no visible flesh pores), and **deletes "realistic photo"**
  (it was pulling the result toward ordinary photographic skin);
- The negative explicitly bans real-human skin cues (`human skin texture / pores / matte skin / 真人皮肤 / 哑光皮肤 / 毛孔`).
