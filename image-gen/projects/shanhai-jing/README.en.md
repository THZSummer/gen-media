# Shan Hai Jing · Baimiao Illustrated Verses (shanhai-jing)

> 🌐 Language: **English** | [中文](README.md)

> Back to [project index](../README.en.md) ｜ Full plan in [PLAN.en.md](PLAN.en.md) ｜ Techniques in [../../skills/README.md](../../skills/README.md)

> A baimiao illustrated-verse series driven by the **verbatim text** of the *Shan Hai Jing*:
> one creature per period, drawn in white-contour ink line, paired with the original passage, its
> volume, and Guo Pu's commentary.
> Built for **Xiaohongshu vertical image posts** (3:4), strictly sourced and character-by-character verifiable.

---

## 1. Project background

- **Name**: `shanhai-jing`
- **Goal**: render every verifiable creature in the *Shan Hai Jing* in the traditional Chinese
  **baimiao + illustrated verse** format, each with its original passage, volume and commentary,
  as a sustainable Xiaohongshu series.
- **Differentiation**: this subject is saturated in AI art, so "looking good" cannot win. The project
  stands on **verifiable scholarship**:
  - every creature carries **volume + original passage + Guo Pu's commentary**, checked word by word
    (source edition in [PLAN.en.md](PLAN.en.md) section 2)
  - **countable traits must be counted correctly** — nine tails means 9 tails, three heads and six eyes
    means 3 heads and 6 eyes; a wrong count fails outright (`fatal`)
  - the title cartouche and the seal are **typeset by tools**, never generated by the model (the model
    was measured drawing garbled seals)
- **Use**: a Xiaohongshu image series (illustrated main plate + source card + series cover)

## 5. Plate craft (after the R5-R8 correction)

```
1) Generation  Z-Image-Turbo plain text-to-image — the "name the mythological being" phrasing (no control image)
2) Typesetting typeset_zanzhi.py: cartouche + source + volume + frame (✅ 5 layout self-checks; seal pending the typeface)
3) Count check crop the tail region, magnify, count by eye and archive it — "reads as nine" is enough, no mechanical certification
4) Scoring     six dimensions A-F (F vitality .25); `fatal` reserved for hard defects
```

**Iron rule**: the model does not write Chinese characters (cartouche / source / seal are typeset with real glyphs).
**Counting**: there is no mechanical-certification gate — making "mechanically certifiable" a hard gate only forces mechanical images ([rounds/r05-review.en.md](subjects/jiu-wei-hu/rounds/r05-review.en.md)).

> WARNING: the first version's process was "ControlNet locks the countable trait -> baimiao generation" and is
> **retired**: measurement showed that what decides success is the **prompt phrasing** (named 8/8 nine-tailed vs
> descriptive 1/6), and that apparatus was machinery for a problem that did not exist.
> `scripts/control_image.py` is kept but demoted and is no longer used for this creature.

**Bone**: **russet colour (gongbi light colour)** as settled in R7. The originally planned baimiao ink-line path
was also validated in R8 (4/4 nine-tailed) but has weaker visual pull, so colour was chosen.

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] `comfyui_gen.py --check` passes
- [ ] the original passage is verified word by word against the source edition, volume correct
- [ ] **the tail count was cropped, magnified, counted by eye and archived** (labelled "visual count, not mechanical certification")
- [ ] cartouche / source / seal are overlaid by tools (`typeset_zanzhi.py`), not generated by the model
- [ ] finals are 1080x1440 (3:4)
- [ ] control entries are marked retired (if a creature has no control image, it used plain text-to-image)
- [ ] `manifest.json` records seed + prompt_id + verbatim prompt
- [ ] finals are stripped (`ffkit strip`)
- [ ] layout self-checks pass (cartouche / key line / passage regions really have ink)
