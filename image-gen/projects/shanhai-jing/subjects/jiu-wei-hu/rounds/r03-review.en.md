## 3. What this round settles

1. **Typesetting can be tooled**: `typeset_zanzhi.py` reads its content from `manifest.json`, no hardcoding -> a new creature only needs a new manifest.
2. **Paper colour must be sampled**, never hardcoded: a hardcoded value produces a visible seam against the plate and looks pasted on.
3. **The plate must be tightened**: the plate carries generous margins, so a plain proportional scale leaves the subject at half the panel width; a content bounding box makes it fill the panel.
4. **Typesetting needs self-checks too**: `signalstats` YMIN mechanically proves "the text really got drawn" at almost no cost, and belongs in the standard typesetting steps.
5. **A known typographic flaw (not hidden)**: in vertical setting, `，` and `。` sit in the centre of the glyph box rather than at the top-right as in classical editions. This is the common treatment for simplified vertical text; strict classical conventions would need separate punctuation positioning.
