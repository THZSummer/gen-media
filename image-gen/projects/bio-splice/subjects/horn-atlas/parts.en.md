**Design**: treat "horns" as an **addable organ** and make an atlas of it ——
**fix a horse that originally has no horns, and grow five kinds of horn on it in turn.**

Unified base = a standard horse (**originally hornless**). **This volume is the sub-theme with the most favourable prior in the whole project.**

## 2. Why this is the sub-theme with "the most favourable prior" in the whole project

Check this repository's five questions / two thresholds one by one:

| Question | Answer |
|------|------|
| ① How fixed is the base overall? | The horse is very fixed —— **but 92 is a whole-body view; the criterion has to look at the landing site** |
| ② Is the landing site a canonical structure? | Top of the forehead / bridge of the nose —— **the horse is originally hornless**, so it is an **empty surface** (Rule 99) |
| ③ Free a structure or an attribute? | No freeing needed (the base does not write horns) |
| ④ Bearing surface? | The surface of the frontal / nasal bone, **growing out of the surface** (Rule 97) |
| Semantic category (Rule 107)? | The five donors **are horns in themselves** (the only slight deviation is the "tusk") |

**All five pass → six kinds of horn, zero failures.** This is an "all conditions satisfied" validation of 92/96/97/99/105/107.

## 3. The small semantic deviations of the "tusk" and the "rhino horn"

- **The narwhal's tusk**: anatomically a tooth, not a horn (the semantics are slightly off), but its form is **a single spiralling long horn** ——
  it holds up completely in testing, which shows that **when form and semantics both hold, form carries a small semantic deviation through**.
- **The rhino horn**: keratinous rather than bony, and it lands on the **bridge of the nose** rather than the top of the forehead ——
  which is exactly what lets the atlas move from "paired horns" to "a single horn, a horn on the nose", the design of this volume's closing.

## 4. Wording discipline

```
✅ a pair of branching deer antlers rising from its forehead   ← 位置短语 + 空面
✅ a single heavy rhinoceros horn rising from its nose
❌ 底座里出现「角」相关的词                                      ← 那就不是空位了（马本来无角）
❌ a horse with an elk's antlers（整只供体）                     ← 规律 57
```
