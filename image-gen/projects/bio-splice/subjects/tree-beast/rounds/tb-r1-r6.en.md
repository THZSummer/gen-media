## 4. Takeaways

105. **"Whether the landing site is a canonical structure" and "what supporting surface this part needs" are two independent thresholds**:
     Beast feet fail both (the root system is a structure + a foot needs joints), beast horns pass both (the trunk is an empty surface + horns grow from the surface).
     → When selecting parts, **both questions must be asked**.
