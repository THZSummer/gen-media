## 2. The finale changes the presentation medium

Period 05 is the project's **first change of presentation medium**: no longer a "field ecology shot" but a **specimen shot**—
a neutral grey background + ring light + full focus, with the framing sentence also switched to the CN prompt `标本照，一个标本独自占据画面：`.

The effect: the larval body covered in mycelium and the stroma tipped with a spore cluster make the image read directly as
**the "cordyceps" entry in the 《Bencao Gangmu》**. → **Changing the medium is the least effortful "upgrade" for a finale**:
with no part changed and only the presentation layer changed, "a good photograph" becomes "a specimen record".
